On camera: esports & gaming host

On-camera hosting for esports and gaming content: live broadcasts and casting, interviews, episode openers, event coverage and branded video. I hosted and cast Day 1 of the European Nations Cup 2026 (Rainbow Six Siege) qualifiers live on Twitch, and hosted street interviews in five Turkish cities for İlave TV, with 3.2M+ total views.

Behind the camera since 2018: live-chat moderation for ESL broadcasts including IEM Katowice, VCT broadcast support, and on-site operations for national tournaments such as Gençlik Kupası. Knowing how a live event runs backstage makes me a calmer host on camera.

Partnerships & growth: esports & iGaming marketing

Finding, pitching and managing creators, streamers and pro players. I reach 300–400 creators and pros a month across Twitch, Kick, YouTube, TikTok and X, in their own language, and follow each deal through to delivery.

Paid social and affiliate growth for gaming and iGaming brands. With GG Clan's media agency I ran campaigns on TikTok, Meta, Reddit, Snapchat and Amazon/Twitch: 1.79M impressions and 258 conversions. In iGaming I managed 50–75 affiliates bringing 150–200 first-time depositors a month.

Outreach at that volume stays personal with AI and automation: I build tools with the Claude API and the Gmail API for research and first drafts, with human approval at every step.

Content: gaming video editing & social media

Short-form and cinematic edits for VALORANT, CS2 and Call of Duty, cut for TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts. The five most-viewed GG Clan TikToks I produced reached 1.86M views. I also script AI-generated launch videos for GG Clan, and my own showreel is built in code with Remotion.

Day-to-day gaming social media management on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Reddit and Telegram: planning, publishing and community replies. I grew Omchain's Telegram from 5K to 9K members and its Instagram from about 950 to 2K followers, and I run GG Clan's channels today.
