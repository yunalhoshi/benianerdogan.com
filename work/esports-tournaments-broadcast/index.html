The challenge

A live esports event has no second take. Matches have to start on time, players need working setups and a stable network, referees need clear rules, and the broadcast has to stay up while the audience watches and chats. Most of that work is invisible when it goes right.

My role

Over four years I worked on both sides of the camera: tournament operations on the ground, and broadcast and community support for international esports streams.

What I did

Esports Specialist · The Academys

Mar – May 2022 · Remote

Tournament operations for Turkey's first esports tournament backed by the Ministry of Youth and Sports, and several national competitions: Gençlik Kupası, the İnce Hesap PUBG Mobile Tournament, the Level Up VALORANT Tournament and the Academys Elite Tournament. The work covered the stage, referees, players, network, OBS and the live stream.
