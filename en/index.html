About me

I'm Benian Erdoğan, also known online as Benian Roden: an on-camera host, gaming content creator and marketer based in Ankara, Türkiye. I studied at Başkent University and work remotely with gaming teams across Europe, MENA and North America.

My work sits where esports, content and growth meet. At GG Clan I run creator and pro-player partnerships, produce the vertical videos behind 1.86M TikTok views, coordinate paid campaigns with GG Clan's media agency and coach CS2 and VALORANT players 1:1. In 2026 I hosted and cast Day 1 of the European Nations Cup (Rainbow Six Siege) qualifiers live on Twitch. Before that I reported on camera for İlave TV, where my street interviews passed 2.5M views on Reels, and spent four years behind the scenes of esports tournaments and broadcasts, from Gençlik Kupası to VCT and IEM Katowice.

I also grew Web3 and iGaming communities on Telegram and Instagram, and I build my own AI tools with the Claude API to take manual work out of outreach and content. If you need someone who can stand in front of the camera, knows the game and ties it all back to numbers, let's talk.
